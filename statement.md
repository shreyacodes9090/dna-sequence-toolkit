-> Problem statement
Doing biological squence , which is important in bioinformatics, manually (spotting mutations , bases counting , translating DNA to protien) , is slow and error prone. thats where my tool comes in.

->Scope 
So my tool can read the DNA squences. Typed in or from a file either way it will read , analyze their composition , translate and transcribe them and compare two squences for mutation.

-> Limitations
This tool does not include a GUI , does not predict protein structure and does not connect to external genomic databases.

->Target users
It would help students in biology or bioenformatics without specialized software wanting to analyze short DNA squence/

-> Module content 
1- Will read or validate DNA squence (typed in or FASTA file)
2- Analyze composition(base counting, convert DNA-> RNA-> protein ) 
3- comparing two sequences and detect mutations
